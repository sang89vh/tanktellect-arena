Tanktellect Arena

Turn-based tank artillery with destructible terrain.

Read the wind, set your angle and power, and launch shells that carve craters into the battlefield. Every shot changes the ground you fight on.

Features

  • Campaign: fight through authored worlds with goals and star rewards.
  • Fortified Assault: command your tank against bunkers, mortars and watchtowers across 18 missions.
  • Duels: play the AI at Easy, Normal or Hard, or share one device in 2-player mode.
  • Modular garage: build tanks from chassis, hull, armour, turret and gun parts.
  • Arsenal: unlock and upgrade 16 weapons.
  • Practice range: test any weapon on targets.

Made for iPhone, iPad and iPhone Duo, in landscape.
